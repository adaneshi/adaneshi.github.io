Research

The working question is from my Columbia thesis. Take a cloud service and an edge device, each as an M/M/1 queue plus a network delay. At the hardware parameters in the draft, the two response times meet near utilization 0.70. Edge is faster only below that root, and the root moves when the service rate or the delay changes.

Federal AI inventories do not record utilization, latency, or a service rate. A row is a filing: the description and the stage an agency wrote down under that year's questions. The paper asks where the hardware condition shows up in those filings. The public draft is SSRN 7141719. I am carrying the same calculation across the 2023, 2024, and 2025 inventories. The project page is here.

Two further papers are in preparation. One asks whether artificial intelligence and automation signals forecast U.S. manufacturing. The other asks whether generative AI moved civic discourse in U.S. notice-and-comment, and whether that movement can be forecast.
